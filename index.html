The Case · Himachal Pradesh
Counting Green Wealth
Himachal Pradesh Forest Department × BIPP–ISB — a future-ready people's forest economy
The Setting

Himachal Pradesh is one of India's most forest-endowed Himalayan states — steep terrain, huge ecological diversity, and forests that are central to climate, hydropower, and rural livelihoods alike.

The Himachal Pradesh Forest Department partnered with BIPP–ISB to build the data and technology infrastructure needed to actually see, measure, and manage this asset — from a single tree to a state-wide economy.

Phase 02 · AI / ML Layer
From Points to Patterns
Multispecies deep learning + Random Forest SDMs, fused with satellite data
What Feeds the Model
  • ~200,000 georeferenced tree points from the field
  • Sentinel-1 (radar) + Sentinel-2 (optical) imagery
  • Canopy height, topography, soil and climate layers
What Comes Out

10m-resolution species distribution maps across the entire state — a working forest intelligence layer, not a static one-time inventory.

Phase 03 · Carbon Estimation
Measuring Carbon, One Tree at a Time
A deep learning model classifies species and measures DBH directly from a field photo
The Pipeline
  • Guard photographs the tree bark and trunk in the field
  • Model classifies the species from the image
  • Model measures diameter at breast height (DBH) from the same photo
  • Species + DBH → biomass → carbon, at tree level

The same 200,000-photo dataset that powers species mapping also becomes the input for a state-wide, tree-level carbon layer — no separate survey required.

Phase 04 · Real Adoption
From Model to Management Plan
Palampur Forest Division was the first to put this data to work
Why This Moment Matters

Species distribution and yield maps generated by the pipeline weren't just published as a report — they were used directly by Palampur block in preparing their own forest management plan.

This is the point the work stopped being a pilot and became something a real forest division relies on to make decisions.

Phase 05 · What's Next
Next Phase : The Forest Intelligence Layer
An HP-specific LLM + deep learning system that never stops learning
The Idea

Rather than a model trained once and left static, this layer continuously refreshes itself — pulling in new forest guard observations and new satellite imagery as they arrive.

Built specifically for Himachal Pradesh's forest governance context, combining LLM-based reasoning with the deep learning models already in place.
